## Frozen domains and future work

- **character**: Logical ID storage is a full 8-bit byte (GameSavePackedUnit.pid); 0x100+ needs a wider ID field across runtime and save, out of pilot scope.
- **class**: GameSavePackedUnit.jid is a 7-bit save bitfield; 0x80 silently truncates to 0x00 on save. Widening needs a save layout/epoch change, out of pilot scope.
- **chapter**: PlaySt.chapterIndex is s8; negatives are reserved sentinels, so the positive range caps at 0x7F. Widening needs a signed->wider save/runtime change.
- **unit**: Unit IDs are partitioned into 0x40-wide faction blocks (FACTION_BLUE/GREEN/RED/PURPLE); a per-faction id at 0x40 collides with the next faction base.
